04 — THE RED PLANET

Mars

Mars is the fourth planet from the Sun and is often called the Red Planet because iron minerals in its soil give its surface a reddish appearance.

Diameter 6,779 km
Distance from Sun 227.9 million km
Day length 24.6 hours
Year length 687 days
Moons 2
Gravity 3.71 m/s²

About Mars

Mars has a thin atmosphere dominated by carbon dioxide. Its surface contains volcanoes, valleys, impact craters and evidence that liquid water existed in the past.

Mars has been extensively explored by spacecraft, including orbiters, landers and rovers.
