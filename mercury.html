Mercury

Mercury is the smallest planet and the closest planet to the Sun.

Property Value
Year 88 Earth days
Day 58.6 Earth days
Radius 2,439.7 km
Diameter 4,879 km
Mass 3.301 × 10²³ kg
Gravity 3.7 m/s²
Temperature -180°C to 430°C
Distance 57.9 million km
Moons 0
Escape Velocity 4.25 km/s
Orbital Speed 47.4 km/s
Atmosphere Very thin (oxygen, sodium, hydrogen, helium, potassium)
Fun Fact: A day on Mercury is longer than its year.
